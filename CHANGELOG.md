# Changelog

All notable changes to MiautifyMobile. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-31

Getting around while music plays.

- Tap the playing song in the player bar to go to its album, artist or folder.
- A new button in the header of the song list and of every album, artist and folder makes
  that list the rest of the queue, while the song playing now carries on.
- Change folder and Refresh moved into a gear menu, on the song list and on the browse screen.
- A Settings screen, with a first setting: show album, artist and folder names in full instead
  of cutting them off.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

The first release: a player for a folder of mp3 files on the phone.

- Choose a music folder; it is remembered between launches.
- The folder is scanned once and cached, so later launches only read new or changed files.
  Pull down on the song list to look for changes.
- Song list with title, artist, album, duration and covers (embedded or a picture in the folder).
- Sort by folder, title, artist, album or most played.
- Browse albums, artists and folders, sorted by most played, recently played, name or
  recently added, and search each of them.
- Playback in the background, with controls in the notification, on the lock screen and on
  headphones.
- Seek bar, shuffle and repeat. Shuffle plays the songs you heard most recently last.
- The queue and the place in the song are remembered between launches.
- Play counts (a song counts after 30 seconds of listening).
- Files that can't play are skipped and marked in red.
